### Business Context

Airbnb operates across different neighbourhoods and offers different types of accommodations. Pricing, availability, reviews, and listing characteristics can vary considerably across locations and property types.

Understanding these differences can help identify patterns in the market and support better pricing, listing, and market-level decisions.

### Business Objective

Analyze Airbnb listings in New York City to understand the factors associated with listing prices, availability, and customer engagement.
